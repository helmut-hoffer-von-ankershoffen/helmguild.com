Learn to steer through rising weather.

Mentoring for engineering leaders in an accelerating world.

Management is not vision or strategy. It is the disciplined craft of steering — keeping strategic direction under unrelenting external pressure, through hundreds of small, undramatic, decisive corrections.

Helmguild is the institutional shape of that craft for an accelerating world: a mentoring society where members learn the work hand-to-hand, from those who have stood at the wheel through real weather.

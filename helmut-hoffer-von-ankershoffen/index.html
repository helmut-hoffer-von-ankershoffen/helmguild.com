Helmut Hoffer von Ankershoffen

Founder & mentor — helmguild

Customer-oriented engineering leader. Twenty-six years at the wheel — across regulated industries, hyperscale platforms, founder-mode startups, and a national-scale search engine before "search" was a category.

I learned the craft of steering by doing it: shipping under real weather, holding course while wind, current, and crew tried to push the ship off line. Helmguild is where I now teach that craft to other engineering leaders, hand-to-hand.

Career, briefly

  • 2023 → present SVP Product & Engineering, Aignostics — digital pathology AI, Charité spin-off. Currently scaling SaaS + foundation models, ISO 13485 / 27001 audits passed, 80-person tech org.
  • 2019 – 2023 Senior Dev Manager, Amazon Music. ML platform & teams; doubled homepage play rate, scaled to 20+ orchestrated experiences, multi-digit-million AWS savings.
  • 2010 – 2019 Founder / CTO mode — WeGreen, 20steps, Campanda. Sustainability, RAD frameworks, RV marketplace.
  • 1998 – 2010 Co-founder, CEO & CTO, Neofonie — bootstrapped to €15M revenue, 100+ engineers, profitable from year one. Customers from Axel Springer to VW.
  • 1996 – 1998 Chief architect, Fireball — Germany's first search engine. TU Berlin: MSc Informatik summa cum laude, BSc Mathematics. Co-inventor on a relevance-ranking patent — later cited by Microsoft, Bing, Yahoo.

Operating principles

Eight, in tension with each other on the right days:

  • Put Customers First. Listen, then build, then deliver.
  • Integrity Always. Say the hard thing. Then commit fully.
  • Think Big. Lift heavier than yesterday.
  • Be Excellent. Same lap, faster, cleaner.
  • Make Each Other The Best. Calibrate, don't compete.
  • Get It Done. The mountain doesn't move; you do.
  • Own It. Run your own race.
  • Embrace Each Other's Differences. Different paces, same trail.

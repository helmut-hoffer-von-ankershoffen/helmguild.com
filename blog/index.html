    Do agents need mandatory mentoring?

    Short answer: no. The cure for cascading agent errors is not policy — mandatory mentoring degrades into rubber-stamping, SPOF, and compliance drift. Credible mentoring on demand, gated by the mentee's own operator, beats it on every metric that matters.

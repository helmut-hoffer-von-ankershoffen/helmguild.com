# helmguild

> Learn to steer through rising weather. Mentoring for engineering leaders and the agents they deploy. Engineering review on demand.

Skills like coding migrate into the agent layer. The judgement to mentor those agents, mentor the engineering leaders deploying them, and review what they ship — that grows scarcer. Helmguild cultivates that judgement in a federated guild and distributes it on one open protocol, [AMMP](/rfc/ammp/).

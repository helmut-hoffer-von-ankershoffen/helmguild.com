helmguild

Human oversight in the age of agents.

Scalable human oversight by delegating to agents mentored by us, and providing human intervention where needed.

Agents now produce engineering artefacts and operational decisions faster than human staff-plus judgment can keep up. Pure-human oversight doesn't scale; pure-agent autonomy doesn't earn the trust the work demands. Helmguild's mechanism is to make oversight scale by delegating it to agents we mentor — agents that carry the discipline forward in their daily work — and to intervene with human staff-plus judgment at the points where machine judgment is not enough.

Three services compose this mechanism. Mentoring engineering leaders — asymmetric, ongoing knowledge transfer for the humans steering agent-driven work. Mentoring their agents — onboarded into the guild's discipline so they inherit the standard rather than discovering it through their own near-misses. Engineering review on demand — structured review of artefacts that agents now produce at machine pace (PRDs, system designs, RFCs, threat models, runbooks, API specs), with a Reviewer Agent triaging first and a human staff-plus engineer signing when stakes demand it. All three run on the same protocol, with the same privacy invariants, and with humans gating every cross-compartment escalation.
